# Sources

Citations for statistics, studies, and third-party materials referenced in Medical Wayfinder's writeup, demo video, and supporting documentation.

## Statistics cited in the pitch

### Cost of wayfinding overhead — "$220,000/year at one tertiary-care hospital"

Zimring, C. M. (1990). *The costs of confusion: Non-monetary and monetary costs of the Emory University hospital wayfinding system.* Atlanta: Georgia Institute of Technology.

Widely cited via:
Ulrich, R. S., Zimring, C., Zhu, X., DuBose, J., Seo, H.-B., Choi, Y.-S., Quan, X., & Joseph, A. (2008). A review of the research literature on evidence-based healthcare design. *HERD: Health Environments Research & Design Journal*, 1(3), 61–125. DOI: [10.1177/193758670800100306](https://doi.org/10.1177/193758670800100306)

### Staff time lost to directions — "~30 minutes/week" and "44% experiencing incivility"

Jamshidi, S., Hashemi, S., & Tran, D.-M. T. (2025). Costs and Effects of Ineffective Wayfinding in US Hospitals: A Survey of Hospital Staff. *HERD: Health Environments Research & Design Journal*, 18(2). DOI: [10.1177/19375867251317240](https://doi.org/10.1177/19375867251317240) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39943920/)

The study reports each staff member spending ~30 min/week assisting with wayfinding, and that ~44% of surveyed staff experienced incivility from users frustrated by wayfinding problems. The five most-asked-about locations were cafeteria, restrooms, elevators, exits, and main lobby.

### Wayfinding pattern data — "3,493 wayfinding requests at one campus"

Sela, R. R., Yan, X., & Lehmann, C. U. (2018). Using Wayfinding Data to Understand Patient Travel Within a Medical Center. *AMIA Joint Summits on Translational Science Proceedings*, 2018, 234–243. [PMC6371236](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6371236/)

(Vanderbilt University Medical Center; Sept 2016 – Sept 2017; 3,493 requests via the VUMC WalkWays app.)

### Limited-English no-show disparities

González Cueto, J. A., Marsh, E. E., Greenwald, J. L., Trinacty, C. M., Lozano, P., & Hardy, M. A. (2024). Disparities in Primary Care No-Shows for Patients with Limited English Proficiency at a Safety-Net Hospital: Retrospective Cohort Analysis. *Journal of General Internal Medicine*, 39, 1234–1240. DOI: [10.1007/s11606-023-08534-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11606-023-08534-4)

### "$150B/year missed-appointment cost"

SCI Solutions report (Q4 2017). Cited across the healthcare-IT trade press:
- [Healthcare Finance News — "Missed appointments cost providers $150 billion annually, report says"](https://www.healthcarefinancenews.com/news/missed-appointments-cost-providers-150-billion-annually-report-says)
- [Healthcare Innovation Group — "Missed appointments cost the U.S. healthcare system $150B each year"](https://www.hcinnovationgroup.com/clinical-it/article/13008175/missed-appointments-cost-the-us-healthcare-system-150b-each-year)

Each missed slot is reported at ~60 minutes / ~$200 in provider time. Treated as industry estimate, not peer-reviewed.

## Data sources

### Facility topology data

Five facilities ship with the submission:

- **Atrius Health Boston Kenmore** (133 Brookline Avenue, Boston, MA)
- **Kaiser Permanente Panorama City Medical Center** (13652 Cantara St, Panorama City, CA)
- **Massachusetts General Hospital** (55 Fruit Street, Boston, MA)
- **Southern Jamaica Plain Health Center** (640 Centre St, Jamaica Plain, MA)
- **Tufts Medical Center** (800 Washington Street, Boston, MA)

For each: building footprint and entrance locations are derived from [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) (ODbL). Department lists, accessibility annotations, and internal landmark prose are authored by the project team from publicly visible signage and the facility's public website.

OSM contributors are credited per ODbL §4.3 by including the line *"© OpenStreetMap contributors"* in any rendering of the facility maps.

### Training data

All 310 training examples in [`training/data/`](training/data/) are **synthetic**, generated by an OpenAI-compatible LLM endpoint against the prompts in [`training/data/prompts/`](training/data/prompts/) and scored by an LLM-as-judge rubric. No real patient queries were used. Anchoring with real queries (50–100 examples) is called out as a V2 dataset improvement in the writeup.

### Eval suite

[`training/data/eval/eval_suite.jsonl`](training/data/eval/eval_suite.jsonl) — 100 cases authored alongside the data contract. Published verbatim with the submission for reproducibility.
